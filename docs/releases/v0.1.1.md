## Included

- Original Text/Column/Row/Card/Button/TextField plus official CheckBox/ChoicePicker/Slider/DateTimeInput/Tabs/Divider.
- Read-only CodeDiff/TestResults with versioned catalog, immutable current IDs and escaped content. TestResults displays supplied Pi provenance and does not independently certify execution.
